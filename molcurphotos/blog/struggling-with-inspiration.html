Navigating the Depths of Photography: Struggling with Inspiration

Molly Curtis December 16, 2023

Photography is a personal creative journey, but self-doubt can sometimes get in the way. As of late 2022, my enthusiasm for photography took a significant hit. I'd love to say it was due to being busy finishing my final year of college — but let's be honest, it was primarily losing motivation, inspiration, and feeling burnt out. And here's the thing: that's completely normal.

Fast forward to today, I've rediscovered my passion for photography and am more motivated than ever. Here are the strategies that helped me get back:

Reflect on Your Journey

Take a moment to appreciate how far you've come. Review your past work and observe the progress you've made. This reflection can reinforce your skills and boost your confidence. The sense of accomplishment from recognizing your own growth can be a powerful motivator — a reminder that your creativity is uniquely yours.

Find a Source of Inspiration

Your inspiration can come from various places — nature, books, other creators, or other hobbies entirely. In my experience, music plays a significant role in driving my creativity. It helps me construct photographic stories and influences my work with vivid imagery in a way that looking at other photography sometimes doesn't.

Learn from Masters

Photography has a rich history filled with iconic artists. Study the work of photographers you admire to gain insight into their techniques and discover new ways of seeing. A few of my favorites right now:

  • Nicholas Fols
  • Niah Aldrich
  • Noah Nash
  • Marco Grob
  • Kimberly

Experiment with New Styles

Don't hesitate to try different styles of photography. Experimenting with various genres can reignite your creative spark — whether it's portrait, landscape, macro, or street photography, exploring new territory leads to fresh inspiration and unexpected breakthroughs.

Connect with a Community

Joining a photography community can provide valuable support and perspective. Sharing your experiences and challenges with other photographers helps dissolve feelings of isolation and self-doubt. Look up local photography groups on Facebook — you might be surprised how many people are working through the exact same thing.

Challenge Yourself

Set photography challenges and personal goals to push your boundaries. Whether it's capturing a specific mood, subject, or technique, creative challenges are exciting and help you grow as an artist. They deepen your connection to the craft and encourage you to discover new approaches.

Seek Education

Consider taking photography classes or workshops. Expanding your knowledge can give you fresh tools and perspectives to work through creative blocks. You might think you know photography well — but there's always room to learn, and you never know what you've limited yourself to without realizing it.

Every photographer faces moments of doubt and lack of inspiration. Embrace those moments as part of your artistic journey.

Your love for photography is limitless. With the right approach, the right people around you, and a little patience with yourself, you can always find your way back to it.
