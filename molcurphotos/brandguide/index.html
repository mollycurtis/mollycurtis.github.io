01 — Introduction

Who is molcurphotos?

Molly Curtis is a nationally published photographer based in Minnesota and Wisconsin. With over seven years of experience across portraits, editorial, senior, weddings, and events — her work is defined by high-contrast light, intentional composition, and the kind of quiet drama that makes an image unforgettable.

Photography isn't documentation. It's interpretation. Every session is a collaboration — structured enough to feel confident, open enough to feel real.

CinematicIntentional High-contrastEditorialIntimate

Drama without artifice

Every image earns its contrast. Shadows are deliberate. Light sources are chosen, not found. The result feels moody because it is — not because a filter was added after.

The subject, always first

Technical craft is invisible in the final image. The only thing that shows is the person — their expression, their energy, their moment. That's the whole point.

Refined restraint

Fewer distractions. Cleaner backgrounds. One focal point. The brand's visual language mirrors this — nothing competes with the photograph itself.

Tone of Voice
Confident, never cold
"I'll guide you through every pose. You don't need to know how to photograph — that's my job."
Direct and reassuring. Eliminate client anxiety by sounding certain.
"Just do whatever feels natural! We'll figure it out together!"
Editorial, not precious
"Your gallery will be delivered in 2–4 weeks. I don't rush what matters."
Premium language. No emoji. No exclamation marks. No hype.
"OMG your gallery is going to be AMAZING!! 🔥✨"
Specific over vague
"Shot on a Sony A7RII with a ZEISS 50mm f/1.4 at golden hour."
Details signal craft and investment. Ambiguity signals mediocrity.
"I use great gear and love all types of photography!"
Never: girlboss, hustle, hype
"If it's not right, we'll reshoot. I stand behind what I deliver."
No overuse of "passion," "dream," or "magic." Let the work speak.
"Living my dream every day capturing your magical memories!! 💫"
02 — Logo

The wordmark

One wordmark. "molcur" in bone white, "photos" in gold — set in Cormorant Garamond italic. No icon. No symbol. The name is the identity, and the color split is the signature.

Gold is the only warmth

All interactive states, active elements, key labels, and logo "photos" suffix. The single warm note. Never used as a background — always as a foreground detail.

Ember is a whisper

Reserved for the rarest decorative moments — a single dot, a hairline, an error state. You notice it last. It shouldn't arrive first.

Bone, not white

Never pure white (#FFF). Bone white is warmer, less clinical, less digital. It recedes slightly against void backgrounds and lets photography breathe.

05 — Imagery

A visual world of contrast

Photographs should feel like stills from a film — intentional light sources, deep shadows, and a subject who fills the frame with presence, not performance.

01

Contrast is the craft

Rich blacks, intentional highlights. Shadows earn their depth. Never flat, never overexposed, never HDR. Drama comes from restraint — not from processing.

02

One focal point

A face, a hand, a gesture. The composition points inward. No busyness in the background. Props only when they add meaning — never for variety.

03

Color: muted & warm

Skin tones pull warm. Backgrounds lean cool or neutral. Never oversaturated. Shadows stay blue-black. Gold is the warmest note in any frame.
