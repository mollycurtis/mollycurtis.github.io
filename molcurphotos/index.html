Molly Curtis is a nationally published photographer based in the Midwest.

With over five years of experience across all types of photography services, and a BBA in Marketing from University of Wisconsin – Eau Claire.

In her free time, Molly enjoys fitness, cooking new recipes, traveling, and hanging out with friends. Learning throughout the years about her style has taught her the value of patience and dedication to art.

Molly is mainly based in Minnesota and Wisconsin but available for projects worldwide.

Reach out via the contact form, email, or Instagram to start discussing details and find a time that works for both of us. To secure your session, a deposit is required upfront with the remainder due on the day of the shoot. I'll send a confirmation a few days in advance to make sure everything runs smoothly.
Every session is unique — rates are tailored to your specific needs, concept, location, and whether a studio is required. Reach out and I'll provide a personalized quote based on what you're looking for.
Turnaround typically ranges from one week to six weeks depending on the session. I aim to deliver the best results and will keep you updated throughout the process so you're never left wondering. The wait is always worth it!
Absolutely! Just let me know after you receive your photos. I archive sessions 2–4 weeks after delivery, so reach out within that window and I can pull additional images for you.
I collaborate occasionally with models I've worked with for a while, typically when there's a specific creative concept or a mutual portfolio-building goal. If you have a unique idea in mind, feel free to reach out and we can talk through the possibilities.
Life happens — I get it. I ask for as much notice as possible (ideally 48 hours) if you need to reschedule due to weather, illness, or anything else. We'll find a new time that works. Please note that cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may incur a cancellation fee.
