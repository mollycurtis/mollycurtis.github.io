Molly Curtis is a nationally published photographer based in the Midwest.

With over seven years of experience across portraits, editorial, weddings, and events, Molly brings a refined, detail-driven approach to her work. She holds a BBA in Marketing and has worked as a digital marketing specialist for the past three years, shaping how she approaches visual storytelling and brand-focused imagery.

Off camera: she enjoys thrifting, decorating, and listening to music. She has a cat named Milo, and loves to travel, with her first solo trip to Japan planned for 2027.

The Zodiac Signs

A twelve-part portrait series inspired by the symbolism, aesthetics, and emotional identities of the zodiac signs. This project combined creative direction, styling, makeup artistry, lighting design, and post-production to create a distinct visual world for each sign.

Creative Process

Before production, I spent months building visual references for each zodiac sign — pulling from mythology, color theory, symbolism, lighting, makeup artistry, wardrobe, and set design. Each sign was approached as its own creative world while still reading as a cohesive collection.

"This project was independently directed and produced before I turned 21 — and it remains one of the most creatively ambitious things I've ever built."

What began as a creative concept evolved into a twelve-part portrait series centered around the symbolism and emotional identity of each zodiac sign. Every shoot was approached with its own visual language through styling, makeup, lighting, color, and atmosphere while still maintaining a cohesive identity across the full series.

This project became one of the most formative creative experiences for me early in my career. Directing and building something at this scale pushed me to think beyond simply taking portraits and more deeply about concept development, storytelling, and creating an immersive visual world around each subject.
