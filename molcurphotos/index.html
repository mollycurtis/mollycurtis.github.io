Molly Curtis is a nationally published photographer based in the Midwest.

With over seven years of experience across portraits, editorial, weddings, and events, Molly brings a refined, detail-driven approach to her work. She holds a BBA in Marketing and has worked as a digital marketing specialist for the past three years, shaping how she approaches visual storytelling and brand-focused imagery.

Off camera: she enjoys thrifting, decorating, and listening to music. She has a cat named Milo, and loves to travel, with her first solo trip to Japan planned for 2027.

The Experience
  • Guided posing throughout your session so you always feel confident and natural
  • Intentionally styled shoots with mood, light, and location chosen to match your vision
  • A collaborative process from inquiry to gallery so you always know what comes next
  • Edits that feel like you, not a filter. Consistent, refined, and true to the moment
Philosophy

"Photography is a way of noticing people. The smallest expressions, environments, and interactions often say the most. My work is centered around creating images that feel honest, intentional, and lasting."
