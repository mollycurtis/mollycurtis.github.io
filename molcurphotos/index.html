Molly Curtis is a nationally published photographer based in the Midwest.

With over seven years of experience in portraits, editorial, weddings, and events, Molly brings a sharp creative eye to every shoot. She holds a BBA in Marketing from UW–Eau Claire, shaping her approach to visual storytelling.

Published in 30+ magazines, her style is built on one idea: every image should feel intentional.

Off camera: thrifting, decorating, traveling (Japan 2027 is locked in), music 24/7, and hanging with her cat Milo.

Based in Minnesota & Wisconsin — available for projects worldwide.
