04

What to
Wear

You don't need a new outfit — you need to feel like yourself. A few simple choices go a long way toward images you'll love for years.

i.
Neutral Tones

Cream, taupe, sage, rust, soft denim, warm white. These photograph beautifully and keep focus on you.

ii.
Coordinate, Don't Match

For groups or couples — choose a color story, not identical outfits. Variety within a palette looks natural.

iii.
Skip the Logos

Large graphics, bold patterns, and neon colors pull the eye away. When in doubt — go simpler.

iv.
Layer + Texture

Scarves, linen, knits, and layering add depth and give us options to work with during the session.

v.
Bring Options

Pack 2–3 looks if you can. Even if we don't use all of them, having choices keeps things relaxed.

vi.
Comfort First

If it doesn't feel like you, it won't feel right in the images. Wear something you genuinely love.
