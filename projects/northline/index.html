The Brief

Launch a product people understand before they want it.

Luggage is usually sold on numbers: capacity, weight, wheel count. But nobody falls for a spec sheet. The real job was to make one bag feel like the answer to every kind of trip you take, then let the details close the deal.

So I built the page as a single, guided story that moves from desire to mechanics to proof, and treats the product as a system you own, not a box you buy once.

The Product Idea

Your trip changes.
Your luggage should too.

One suitcase. Different ways to use it.

Most carry-ons are a hollow shell you fill with loose packing cubes that shift the moment you lift the bag. ONE starts with the Passage Frame, a lightweight aluminum structure that stays in the case for good.

Everything else clips onto the Frame and changes with the trip. Reconfigure in seconds without unpacking. Lift a module out to carry it on its own.

i

The Frame stays. The permanent interior spine every kit mounts to.

ii

The kits change. Weekender, Work, Field, Long Haul, swapped in and out.

iii

Nothing floats. Everything locks down, so a full bag still feels like one solid object.

Choose how yours travels

One suitcase, configured for you.

    Designed to keep up

    The bag does some of the work.

    Three features that make ONE feel less like luggage and more like a travel companion, and give the landing page a reason to talk about intelligence, not just materials.

    i
    It follows you.
    A low, quiet drive system lets ONE move on its own, staying a step behind you through the terminal and stopping when you stop. Hands free for the boarding pass and the coffee.

    ii
    You always know where it is.
    Location tracking is built into the Frame, not clipped on as an afterthought. Open the Passage app and ONE is on the map, in the terminal, in the hold, or three carousels over.
