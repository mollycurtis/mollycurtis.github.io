The Brief

Launch a product people understand before they want it.

Luggage is usually sold on numbers: capacity, weight, wheel count. But nobody falls for a spec sheet. The real job was to make one bag feel like the answer to every kind of trip you take, then let the details close the deal.

So I built the page as a single, guided story that moves from desire to mechanics to proof, and treats the product as a system you own, not a box you buy once.

The Product · Choose how yours travels

One suitcase. Different ways to use it.

Every PASSAGE is built on the Frame, a lightweight aluminum structure that stays in the case for good. The kits clip onto it and change with the trip, so you reconfigure without unpacking. At checkout you don't pick a color, you pick how it's packed.Design decision 01. Lead with the suitcase, not the spec sheet. Buyers commit to the trip they picture.

    Designed to keep up

    The bag does some of the work.

    Three features that make PASSAGE feel less like luggage and more like a travel companion, and give the landing page a reason to talk about intelligence, not just materials.

    i
    It follows you.
    A low, quiet drive system lets PASSAGE move on its own, staying a step behind you through the terminal and stopping when you stop. Hands free for the boarding pass and the coffee.
    ii
    You always know where it is.
    Location tracking is built into the Frame, not clipped on as an afterthought. Open the Passage app and PASSAGE is on the map, in the terminal, in the hold, or three carousels over.

    iii
    It knows the rules.
