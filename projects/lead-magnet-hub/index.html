The Brief

Build NBI's case to be the #1 destination for legal AI training.

AI is changing how attorneys work, and NBI had the courses to help — but no single place pulling it all together. Before this hub, someone searching for AI legal training would land on a general course catalog with no clear path forward.

The goal was to build a page that worked at every stage: give something free to attorneys who weren't ready to pay, make it easy for interested ones to find the right course, and push high-intent visitors toward a subscription.

The Opportunity

A gap in the market and a gap on the site.

AI legal education is a rapidly growing category. Law firms are asking attorneys to get up to speed — and those attorneys need somewhere credible to start. NBI had everything needed to own that space. Four things were missing.

1
No dedicated entry point
AI courses were spread across the catalog with no hub to surface them. A lawyer searching for "AI CLE" had no obvious starting point on the site.
2
No free content to capture early-stage visitors
Attorneys just starting to explore AI weren't ready to pay. Without free resources, NBI lost them before they could become customers.
3
No positioning statement
Competitors were starting to build AI training pages. NBI needed a clear, prominent claim to establish itself first — before the category got crowded.
4
No full-funnel path in one place
From awareness to conversion, attorneys had to piece together NBI's AI offering from multiple pages. One page needed to do all of it.

Strategy

Meet the attorney wherever they are in the journey.

01

Lead with the positioning claim

The hero states "NBI is the #1 destination for AI training for lawyers" before anything else. Attorneys need a reason to trust the source before they'll engage with the content below it.

02

Give something free before asking for money

A lawyer who just searched "AI for attorneys" isn't ready to buy a CLE course. The free resources section — webinars, a quiz, an on-demand video — gives them a reason to engage with NBI first. Without this, they bounce.

03

Show the course catalog once they're warmed up

After a visitor has watched a free webinar or taken the quiz, they're more open to seeing paid options. The featured courses section comes after the free resources on purpose — sequence matters.

04

Push high-intent visitors to subscribe, not just buy one course

The subscription upsell sits after the course catalog — at the moment someone has already seen the depth of what NBI offers. A subscription is a better outcome than a single purchase, so it gets placed where visitors are most likely to say yes.

05

Keep the page useful for visitors who aren't ready yet

The blog section at the bottom serves attorneys who need more time. It keeps the page useful, builds familiarity with NBI, and supports organic search — bringing in attorneys who weren't specifically looking for NBI to begin with.

What the Hub Delivers
One page. Every stage of the funnel.

Conversion

Positioning

Takeaway

Every free resource on this hub exists to move an attorney one step closer to a course purchase or a subscription.
