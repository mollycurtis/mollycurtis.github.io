Test it. Read it. Test it again.

Three A/B tests for an online course subscription business, spanning paid search, the subscription checkout, and the homepage. Some variants won, some lost, and both outcomes shaped the next test.

01 · Paid Search

Nine states. One template. Nine answers.

Every state ran its own paid campaign against one shared page. The redesigned template was tested market by market, since no two states convert the same way.

The losses
Illinois and West Virginia already converted well, and the redesign replaced what those markets responded to. A high baseline was a warning, not a target.
The wins
Kentucky and New York started lowest and gained most. The template fixed pages that weren't working, not ones that already were.
What changed after
Winners rolled out. Losers reverted and were re-tested on their own, rather than forcing one template onto every market.
02 · Subscription
