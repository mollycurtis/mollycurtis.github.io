Hi, I’m Molly.

“Photographer, marketer, and the friend with the good eye, in one person.” Based in St. Paul, working across the Twin Cities.

stylist
photographer
thrifter
For people who have a space but don’t know how to make it feel like them.

I have spent my whole career making things look intentional. As a marketer I build brands, which is really just deciding what a thing should feel like and making every detail agree. As a photographer I read light and composition for a living. Interior styling is where those two habits finally live in the same room.

My actual obsession is the hunt. Estate sales at 8am, the back corner of the thrift store, the Marketplace listing with the terrible photo hiding a great chair. Most of what I use is thrifted, sourced, or already yours, which keeps budgets sane and keeps your home from looking like a showroom.

Then the two backgrounds pay off. The marketing brain builds a clear direction so the whole space feels like one idea. The photographer’s eye styles it down to the last object and shoots it properly when it is done, so it looks as good in a photo as it does in person.

If beige is your thing, I can absolutely do a beautiful warm and layered version of it. The point is never a specific palette. The point is that your home ends up feeling like you.
